What AInsight does

An AI analyst you can actually check

Conversation on the surface, validated analytics underneath.

Ask Your Data

Ask questions in natural language instead of writing SQL or manually calculating spreadsheets.

Agentic Analysis

AInsight decides which analytical tools and operations are required to answer your question.

Trusted Numbers

Calculations are performed with analytics engines such as pandas and SQL and validated before they are shown. The AI never does the arithmetic.

Interactive Visualizations

Generated charts can be explored, expanded, downloaded and questioned: ask AInsight about the chart you are looking at.

Business Insights

Multiple Data Sources
